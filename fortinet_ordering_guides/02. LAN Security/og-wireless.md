Ordering Guide
FortiAP
Welcome to the Fortinet FortiAP Ordering Guide. This guide provides a clear overview
of the options to help teams select the appropriate Wi-Fi 6E and Wi-Fi 7 FortiAP solution
based on deployment size, user density, performance requirements, and operational
needs. It outlines key considerations for building secure, high-performance Wi-Fi
networks that are centrally managed and seamlessly integrated within the Fortinet
Security Fabric.
• The latest FortiAP portfolio delivers enterprise-class wireless connectivity built on Wi-
Fi 7 (IEEE 802.11be), enabling higher throughput, lower latency, Multi-Link Operation
(MLO), and improved performance in high-density environments. Combined with
centralized management through FortiGate, FortiManagement Cloud, or FortiSASE,
FortiAPs provide secure, scalable wireless access that is fully integrated with the
Fortinet Security Fabric.
• Rather than selecting an access point based solely on hardware specifications, this
guide aligns the FortiAP portfolio with common customer deployment personas to
simplify product selection and sizing.
• The deployment categories covered in this guide include:
• Remote Worker / Home Office – Single access point deployments supporting up to
25 users, optimized for secure remote connectivity, Zero-Touch Provisioning, and
cloud-managed operations.
• Small Branch / Retail – One to two access points supporting approximately 25–75
users, delivering reliable Wi-Fi coverage with simple deployment, integrated security,
and centralized management.
• Enterprise Branch / SMB / Campus – Medium to large deployments ranging from
a handful of access points to thousands across distributed locations. These
environments support hundreds to tens of thousands of users and require high-
performance Wi-Fi, centralized policy management, automation, AI-assisted
troubleshooting, and seamless scalability.
• High-Density Environments – Auditoriums, education, healthcare, hospitality, and
large public venues where high client density, multi-gigabit performance, and
advanced RF capabilities are essential.
• Outdoor & Industrial (OT) – Ruggedized deployments for manufacturing, warehouses,
logistics, utilities, transportation, and outdoor campuses, where resilient wireless
connectivity, environmental durability, and integration with industrial networks are
critical.
For each deployment persona, this guide recommends the most appropriate FortiAP
models, licensing options, power requirements, mounting accessories, and management
platform to streamline ordering and ensure successful deployments.

Product Offerings
FortiAP wireless access points deliver secure, high-performance Wi-Fi as an integral part of the Fortinet Security Fabric.
Managed centrally via a FortiGate firewall or FortiManagement Cloud, they offer unified visibility and control. FortiAPs provide
advanced security features like threat protection, identity-based access, and application control, ensuring robust wireless
connectivity. This integration simplifies management while extending Fortinet’s comprehensive security to your wireless network.
