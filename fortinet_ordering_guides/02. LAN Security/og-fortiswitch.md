Welcome to the Fortinet FortiSwitch Ordering Guide. This guide is designed to serve as a
comprehensive reference for Fortinet’s secure access switch solutions, which are built to
deliver outstanding security, performance, and manageability. These Ethernet switches
are reliable, highly-performing, and purpose-built to address a wide range of needs, from
small office access layers to large-scale data centers.
This guide outlines:
• The tight integration of FortiSwitch with the Fortinet Security Fabric via FortiLink.
• Centralized management capabilities from the FortiGate interface.
• Key features of FortiSwitch models, including PoE, FPoE, MultiGig, and MCLAG.
• Product offerings for various use cases, such as secure access and secure campus
environments.
• The availability of FortiSwitch Rugged models for specific deployment needs.
