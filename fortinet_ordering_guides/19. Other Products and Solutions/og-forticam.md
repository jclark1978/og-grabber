Fortinet video surveillance solutions are designed to help organizations prevent loss, ensure
workplace safety, and optimize operational efficiency. Flexible deployment options—hardware
appliances, virtual machines, and cloud—Fortinet delivers secure, easy-to-manage, and scalable
security surveillance. This smart solution is backed by Fortinet’s leading security and AI technology
to ensure the highest level of reliability and protection. Organizations of all sizes can find tailored
security surveillance to meet their unique requirements and budget.
Product Offers
Fortinet’s AI-driven video surveillance solution combines smart FortiCamera, high-performance
FortiRecorder NVRs, and feature-rich FortiCentral video management software. It is available as an
easy-to-use Cloud platform, as On-prem solution or in a Hybrid configuration combining the best
of both. This intelligent secure surveillance is fully integrated with the Fortinet Security Fabric,
enabling organizations to simplify management and visibility across all locations.
FortiCamera model series for cloud, analytics or recorders delivers rich security coverage with
superior image quality in all lighting conditions. From compact indoor units to weatherproof
outdoor models, it features real-time monitoring, advanced motion detection and intelligent
analytics to give you peace of mind. AI-powered analytics for faces and vehicles give organizations
visibility to detect suspicious activities in their premises.
FortiRecorder network video recorders deliver high-performance capacities for continuous
video recording, secure storage and scalable camera supports, ensuring reliable surveillance,
streamlined management, and seamless integration across your security infrastructure.

Frequently Asked Questions
How can I extend storage on FortiRecorder?
• The FortiRecorder appliance has HD bays that you can fill with replacement disks.
• For VM instances it depends on the amount of storage that the host platform provides.
• You can add external storage as NAS via NFS or iSCSI. USB is available on the appliance models for very small installations.
What is FortiCentral?
FortiCentral is the FortiCamera free video management system. It offers a range of professional features for manned and
forensic video surveillance.
The FortiCentral application is available for download.
Is there a sizing guide for VM installations? FortiRecorder?
Although you can stack VM licenses for up to 1000 cameras, this is a theoretical limit due to the host platform CPU and
bandwidth constraints. Practically, partitioning larger installations into clusters of <250 cameras is best practice. For information
and sizing guidelines, contact the FortiCamera CSEs.
How can I view video from FortiCameras?
• FortiRecorder interface
• FortiCentral video management software
• FortiCamera Mobile app (Android or iOS)
• API
• FortiCamera Cloud portal (available for active FortiCamera Cloud licenses)
What licensing do I need for FortiCamera Cloud?
Each camera available in FortiCamera Cloud portal requires an active annual subscription. You can order FC1-10-FCCLD-518-01-
DD for any number of units and in 1-3-5 year terms.
FortiRecorder supports a hybrid mode in 7.6.0+ where cameras connected to a FortiRecorder are available and managed through
the FortiCamera Cloud portal.
For details about FortiCamera Cloud licensing, see the portal guide.
Does FortiCamera Cloud store data in the cloud?
All videos are stored in FortiCamera Cloud cameras for fast access. For a hybrid model, you can store videos in an onsite
FortiRecorder NVR.
Does FortiCamera Cloud offer video analytics?
FortiCamera FD55-CA, FB85-CA , and FE120B-CA are cloud AI cameras performing edge-based person, face, and vehicle
detection. FortiCameraCloud portal supports searching for people and faces based on appearance or attributes. Business
statistics like footfall counting and heatmaps are offer detailled insight into customer traffic. FB85-CA has license plate
recognition enabled.
