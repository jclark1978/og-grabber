Enterprise Agreement Program
Enterprise Agreements (EAs), cost savings, and efficiencies are extended
to support, services, and licensing. Without an EA, organizations
purchase licenses and subscriptions on a per-unit basis. This method
requires a lot of time to manage licenses and understand whether proper
licensing is in place with every vendor. EAs are common buying programs
in the technology industry and our competitive offering places us in a
strategic position.
What is Fortinet’s Enterprise Agreement?
The Fortinet EAs offer account-based, comprehensive support and
licenses for both the existing estate and projected purchases. This
method makes it ideal for large and/or growing security infrastructures.
Fortinet offers three options: Enterprise Support Agreement, Fabric
Enterprise License Agreement, and Endpoint Enterprise License
Agreement. They cover all existing products and projected purchases
during the term. The term length can either be one, three, or five years
with growth allowance.
