Ordering Guide
FortiSAT
Security Awareness Training & Phishing Simulation
Reduce Human Risk. Strengthen Your Security Posture.
Fortinet’s FortiSAT provides a unified, human-centric approach to cybersecurity.
Together, security awareness training and phishing simulation help organizations
educate users, simulate phishing attacks, and empower them to wrap tighter security
around high risk users.
Platform Highlights
A role-based, compliance-aligned platform that empowers employees with essential
cybersecurity knowledge:
• 10+ languages with accessibility support
• Helps meet NIST, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, GDPR compliance requirements
• Comprehensive library of training modules including micro and nano learning
modules
• Auto-enrolls users that fail phishing campaigns into remedial training
Phishing simulations that test user readiness and measure risk in real-world conditions:
• Realistic email and QR code phishing simulations
• Behavioral risk scoring
• Utilize risk scores in FortiMail, FortiDLP and other cybersecurity products to wrap
tighter policies around high risk users
• Comprehensive library of phishing email templates (including landing pages)
• Template builder allows for custom phishing email and landing page templates
