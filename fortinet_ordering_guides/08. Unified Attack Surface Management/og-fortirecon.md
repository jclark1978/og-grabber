FortiRecon
FortiRecon is a Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM) service that allows customers
to gain visibility of their digital attack surface, receive targeted threat intelligence, and reduce
organizational risk.
The solution consists of five main components:
Attack Surface Management (EASM and IASM)
The External Attack Surface Management (EASM) module provides information about your digital
assets, potential security issues, and leaked credentials. It can be used to identify exposed known
and unknown assets, learn about associated vulnerabilities, and prioritize the remediation of
critical issues.
The Internal Attack Surface Management (IASM) module provides comprehensive internal asset
discovery, vulnerability assessment, and web application analysis. It allows you to scan multiple
subnets and deploy the IASM Agent across multiple sites to ensure complete visibility into
your internal attack surface. IASM Agent is a Docker container deployed within your network,
responsible for executing scans and relaying discovered data to the FortiRecon.
Brand Protection (BP)
BP continually monitors the organization’s external brand reputation for typo squatting, rogue
applications, and impersonation via web sites and social media, which may impact brand value,
integrity, and trust. The service monitors high value targets within the organization using Executive
protection to identify loss of personal information which may be used by threat actors in targeted
attacks.
The Brand Protection (BP) module uses proprietary algorithms to detect common techniques used
by cyber threat actors, such as web-based phishing attacks, typo-squatting, defacements, rogue
apps, credential leaks, and brand impersonation in social media. It can be used to detect activity
early and take action, such as web site or application takedowns, to protect your brand’s value,
trust, integrity, and reputation.
Adversary Centric Intelligence (ACI)
ACI leverages FortiGuard Threat Research Team to provide organization-specific and expertly
curated Dark Web, open source, and technical threat intelligence including threat actor insights.
The approach enables organizations to proactively assess risks, respond faster to incidents, better
understand their attackers, and protect assets.
Red Teaming (RT)
Red Teaming assessment provides realistic, controlled emulation of how an attacker would breach
an organization. It uses adversary tactics, techniques, and behaviors observed in real-world
attacks to breach an organization’s IT environment. It produces validated end-to-end attack paths
from external entry to defined objectives.
Security Orchestration (SO)
Security Orchestration lets teams investigate and respond to security threat findings from
FortiRecon by automating and streamlining security workflows using predefined and custom
playbooks.

Frequently Asked Questions
What defines an asset?
An asset is any monitored resource, such as an ASN, IP address, domains, subdomains, or certificates. How can my customer
find out the number of assets they have? Work with the Fortinet sales specialist to help scope the number of assets.
How can my customer find out the number of assets they have?
Work with the Fortinet sales specialist to help scope the number of assets.
What is the new security orchestration feature and how can customers trial it?
The Security Orchestration module helps customers investigate and respond to security threat findings from FortiRecon’s
Attack Surface Management, Brand Protection, and Adversary Centric Intelligence modules. FortiRecon provides 100 playbook
executions per month to begin using the Security Orchestration module. For additional executions please purchase an add-on
SKU.
Are SKUs stackable?
Assets are licensed up to an asset count and are not stackable. To jump between ranges, you must coterm. Service credits for
takedown, executive monitoring and vendor risk assessment are stackable.
How do I increase the service level or number of assets after the initial order?
Contact Customer Support who can advise on the upgrade process.
How do I extend the contract period?
FortiRecon is a term-based license; therefore, it is possible to stack contract licenses to an existing subscription to extend the
term. Contact Customer Support who can advise on the process.
How do I calculate asset sizing?
Each active asset (AS number, IP, domain, sub-domain) which is being monitored is chargeable as a full asset. Assets that
are scanned but non-responsive are chargeable as 1/10 of an asset. This charge can be removed by excluding non-active IP
ranges if necessary.
