Overview
FortiGuard Advisory and Incident Response Services play a crucial role in assisting
organizations to assess and improve their current security posture while effectively
responding to digital threats through advanced digital forensics and incident response
capabilities. These services provide organizations with expert insights into their existing
security landscape, identifying vulnerabilities and weaknesses that may be exploited by
cyber threats.
FortiGuard Incident Response Subscription Service
Today’s threat actors are continuously innovating, creating more sophisticated attacks
and methods of delivery. The reality is that security breaches are inevitable. At some
point, every organization is faced with a security incident that needs fast investigation,
fast response, and/or fast remediation.
Unfortunately, a large number of organizations may not have the skilled resources to
properly prepare for and take care of a security incident when the time comes to act
quickly. This typically results in more severe damage and higher business impact than
when there is better preparation and an immediate expert response to a detected
incident.
To fill this gap, Fortinet offers security leaders the ability to better prepare, rapidly
respond, and take the most effective actions at every step, with FortiGuard Incident
Readiness Subscription Service.

Detailed Service Information
Respond
In the event of a security incident, FortiGuard’s skilled incident response team swiftly engages in digital forensics and incident
response to investigate and contain the threat. By leveraging their deep expertise, they help organizations mitigate the
immediate impact of an attack, minimize potential data loss, and understand the root cause.
Ultimately, FortiGuard Advisory and Incident Response Services empower organizations to proactively strengthen their defenses
and respond effectively to the ever-evolving threat landscape, enhancing overall cybersecurity resilience. This is done through
the development of incident response plans and playbooks and via testing these via tabletop exercises.
Digital Forensics and Incident Response
DFIR provides help to organizations in the midst of a cybersecurity incident, including targeted ransomware attacks.
Experienced staff, expert skills, powerful tools, and established process are used to efficiently assess the situation, its scope,
and steps necessary to contain the impact and help recover operations. Fortinet provides DFIR to help organizations effectively
respond to and recover from security incidents and breaches. DFIR includes:
• Incident Detection and Analysis: Fortinet’s service helps organizations identify and analyze security incidents, such as data
breaches, malware infections, and other cyber threats. This may involve real-time monitoring, analysis of security logs, and
the use of threat intelligence.
• Forensic Investigation: Incident response often includes forensic analysis to determine the scope, cause, and impact of a
security incident. Fortinet’s service may involve digital forensics to collect and analyze evidence related to the incident.
• Containment and Eradication: Fortinet assists organizations in containing and eradicating the threat, preventing further
damage, and ensuring the incident does not spread further within the network.
• Documentation and Reporting: Fortinet assists in documenting the incident, the actions taken during the response, and the
lessons learned. This information can be valuable for compliance, legal, and internal purposes.
It’s essential to note that the specific services and offerings may vary based on an organization’s needs, the type and scale of
the incident, and the terms of the engagement with Fortinet. Incident response services are critical for organizations looking to
effectively manage and mitigate the impact of security incidents and breaches.

Incident Response Plan Development
This Service Option provides assistance to the Customer in the development of an organization-wide Incident Response Plan
(IRP). Incident response plans are a foundational part of security hygiene, especially as the threat landscape continues to evolve.
These plans are designed to provide decision-makers and pertinent staff with the steps to take when an incident strikes, as
well as who to communicate with during and after the incident. This custom document will include details on the IR team, IR
communications, and details on key areas of the IR life cycle, including:
• Preparation
• Detection
• Analysis
• Containment and eradication
• Recovery
• Post-incident activity

Incident Response Playbook Development
This Service Option provides assistance to the Customer in the development of a step-by-step playbook to be used in the
event of an impactful cyber security incident on its network based on the most likely incidents. This playbook is meant to help
Customer’s security analysts to handle a security incident from detection through eradication and recovery and may be part of
an organization’s larger incident response plan. Some of the current probable events may include:
• A ransomware attack,
• Phishing email messages,
• A compromised user’s credentials.

Red Team Assessment Services
The Red Team Assessment offers a realistic assessment of an organization’s security posture by simulating real-world
attack scenarios. This not only helps in identifying vulnerabilities that traditional assessments might miss but also tests the
effectiveness of both technical defenses and human responses. Through a customer developed assessment Fortinet looks
to identify vulnerabilities within an organizations people, processes and technology via a tailored approach. This service is
delivered as either Objective-Led Red Teaming or Training-Led Red Teaming.
Objective-Led Red Teaming is a structured approach to red teaming that focuses on achieving specific objectives or goals
in a deliberate and purpose-driven manner. The purpose of Objective-Led Red Teaming is to provide organizations with a
strategic and targeted assessment of their capabilities, defenses, and preparedness in the face of specific threats or challenges.
Examples include bypassing security mechanism in place in a restricted network segment in order to exfiltrate sensitive date
or compromised end user credential via spear phishing attacks and then leverage those credentials to remotely access the
network.
Training-Led Red Teaming is a specific approach to red teaming that primarily serves an educational or training purpose.
The primary purpose of Training-Led Red Teaming is to train and develop organizational personnel, by simulating real-world
adversarial activities and threats in a controlled environment. During such an exercise the red team would collaborate with the
organization to share insights and defensive response strategies.
Fortinet will work with the customer determine their goals of the assessment and develop a custom approach to best fit the
goals of the organization. As such, this Service Option is subject to a scoping call leading to a plan of action associated to a

Security Operations Center (SOC) Development Service
This Service Option is a custom-tailored service to collaborate with your organization to establish a resilient and proficient
Security Operations Center (SOC). This is achieved by directing efforts towards four fundamental pillars crucial to any SOC:
Organization, Visibility, Response, and Evolution.
Organization: This area of focus addresses the cohesion of structures both outside and inside the SOC, encompassing the
alignment of the SOC with the business, the internal organization of the SOC itself, and its integration into the broader Incident
Response framework.
Visibility: Detecting malicious activities within the organization is a pivotal aspect of the SOC, necessitating the implementation
of appropriate tools, logging mechanisms, use cases, and intelligence.
Response: An effective SOC demands the establishment of well-defined processes, playbooks, workflows, data sharing
mechanisms, communications protocols, and the deployment of skilled personnel. Visibility holds little value without an
adequate and timely response.
Evolution: The SOC necessitates continuous maturation and development, requiring meticulous planning, metrics, training
programs, exercises, and assessments to be consistently upheld. A SOC that attains a certain level of maturity but stagnates will
rapidly lose its efficacy, given that attackers evolve daily. This makes the Evolution pillar indispensable for the ongoing success
of the SOC.
With the assistance of the customer, Fortinet will develop a custom offering which will focus on the needs of the organization.
This may include assessing the organization current maturity, developing organizational process and procedures, aligning
the SOC with the goal of the business, ensuring appropriate people, processes, and technology are in place, measuring
improvement and more.

Active Directory Security Assessment
This Service Option provides a third-party, objective, review of the security posture of an AD installation. It helps to identify
critical issues and areas of the highest concern. It also provides the organization a means for tracking the continuing
improvement and maturity of the Active Directory security posture.
The FortiGuard Active Directory Security Assessment is organized in five areas of focus that each incorporate people,
processes, and technology. Each of the areas consists of a number of maturity practices that are used to assess the AD
installations security and fit for purpose within the larger business mission, current threats, and capacity to evolve efficiently
over time.
FOCUS AREA
This area starts with governance and basic procedures that are derived from the goals and objectives of the governance policies. The focus will be
Policy and Procedures ensuring that your AD installation has proper executive backing and resources, as well as basic procedures that ensure the environment is ready for
adverse events and incident response.
This area addresses account management policies, procedures, and security settings which are derived from various standards bodies and
Account Management
Microsoft publications. Many issues addressed in this section are considered to be critical to the security of AD and your IAM program.
AD hosts are high value targets for threat actors and need to be hardened. In addition, based on its utility and design, AD is frequently deployed
Network and Host Configuration
redundantly and to multiple locations within the organization. This section addresses both network and host security configuration issues.
In order to ensure visibility for auditing and investigation, default audit configurations need to be verified, and specific audit flags may need to be
Audit Configuration set. If proper auditing is not enabled then information will not be collected, and critical questions about access and activities may not be able to be
answered. This section covers the most important audit settings based on both Microsoft and standards bodies recommendations.
Because AD and Administrator accounts are high value targets for threat actors, continuous monitoring of some critical AD events needs to be
Monitoring
implemented. This section reviews the most critical events which should be monitored and reviewed for legitimacy and authorization.
The assessment operates at a higher level of policy, procedure, and configuration. The detailed security settings of individual
accounts are not reviewed, only settings and permissions for privileged and sensitive users are groups are reviewed.

Penetration Testing Services

applications to identify unknown vulnerabilities that an adversary could exploit. Penetration testing mimics real-world attacks
