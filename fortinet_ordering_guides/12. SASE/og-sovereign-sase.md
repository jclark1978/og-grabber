FortiSASE Sovereign
FortiSASE Sovereign is a turnkey solution for customers who aim to deliver private
SASE services for internal or external consumption. It is designed for large enterprises,
federal agencies, and service providers looking to deliver private SASE services.
FortiSASE Sovereign provides an alternative to public SASE offerings, such as FortiSASE,
which are hosted in vendor datacenters. In contrast, FortiSASE Sovereign is designed for
deployment on customer-controlled infrastructure, ensuring that security inspection and
data sovereignty remain entirely within your organization’s boundaries and control.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - FORTISASE SOVEREIGN
Is FortiSASE Sovereign a SASE service?
FortiSASE Sovereign is not a standalone SASE service. It is designed as a turnkey solution to enable an organization or service
provider to provide their own, customizable SASE services.
How does the responsibility model differ for FortiSASE Sovereign?
FortiSASE Sovereign uses the same core Fortinet technologies as the traditional FortiSASE service but gives the customer
greater control over infrastructure placement and day-to-day operations. Depending on the selected deployment model, certain
responsibilities may remain with Fortinet or shift to the customer, with guidance and support from Fortinet.

How much control does an organization have when deploying FortiSASE Sovereign?
FortiSASE Sovereign gives organizations greater control over where the SASE platform is deployed, where security inspection
occurs, and where logs and operational data are stored. Customers can use Fortinet-hosted components for a more turnkey
experience or deploy the orchestration, security, and analytics components within customer-controlled infrastructure for a fully
on-premises model.
Which deployment model is right for me?
The appropriate deployment model depends on your operational, privacy, and data-sovereignty requirements. The Cloud
Orchestrator provides the simplest and most turnkey option for most organizations, while the on-premises Virtual Appliance
is intended for customers that require the orchestration platform, security services, and associated data to remain within
customer-controlled infrastructure.
What FortiSASE Sovereign components can be deployed on premises?
The orchestration platform, security inspection components such as FortiGate, and logging and analytics through FortiAnalyzer
can all be deployed within the customer environment to support a fully sovereign, on-premises architecture. When ordering this
deployment model, select the Virtual Appliance option, all required orchestration subcomponents, and the corresponding user
licenses specifically designed for the on-premises deployment.
Can user licenses purchased for the Cloud deployment be used with the on-premises deployment?
No. The SaaS and on-premises deployment models use separate user license SKUs that are tied to their respective
orchestration platforms. Customers deploying the on-premises Virtual Appliance must purchase the user licenses specifically
designated for that model; existing SaaS user licenses cannot be transferred or reused

Can FortiSASE Sovereign work with FortiSASE in a hybrid solution?
No, FortiSASE Sovereign cannot be combined or used with FortiSASE.
It is designed as a sovereign solution, to be hosted in customer-controlled infrastructure. Organizations who wish to deliver
their own private SASE service should ensure they have the infrastructure capacity to host the security inspection and data
sovereignty appliance. For customers who do not wish to host any appliance, Fortinet offers hosted appliance solutions, such as
FortiGate and FortiAnalyzer, that could be rented on a monthly basis.
Do I need to purchase additional FortiGate licenses, such as FortiGuard services or VDOM licenses?
No. The FortiSASE Sovereign FortiGate entitlement includes the required FortiGuard security services and VDOM licensing. No
additional FortiGuard or VDOM licenses are required for FortiGate devices used as Sovereign SASE Security POPs.
Could the FortiGate (security inspection appliance) be hosted in Fortinet datacenters?
FortiGate-as-a-Service (FGaaS), is the industry’s first Hardware-as-a-Service option that combines the power of our proprietary
ASIC technology with the flexibility and scalability of cloud services. This service can be used with FortiSASE Sovereign to utilize
Fortinet resources for security inspection. FortiAnalyzer-as-a-Service will be available in a future release.
Who controls the design and architecture of FortiGate and Security Fabric devices?
While Fortinet provides best practice design and topologies, we give the customer autonomy over their design and deployment
for their SASE service. Customers can choose how many FortiGates to deploy according to their intra-datacenter and inter-
datacenter failover requirements.
How do I get started with a trial or evaluation?
For evaluation and testing purposes (non-production use), we recommend ordering designated Not For Resale (NFR) SKUs
through your partner. These SKUs are purpose-built and heavily discounted to support product trials and validation.
The following components are recommended to get started:
• FortiSASE Sovereign Orchestration EVAL License: FC-10-OVSAN-1081-02-DD
• FortiSASE Sovereign 25 User License: FC-10-EMSSN-552-02-DD (Quantity of 4 max)
• FortiGate 91G or 901G (SSNFR version): FG-91G-SSNFR or FG-901G-SSNFR
• Any FortiAnalyzer appliance with firmware 7.4 or above
SSNFR and EVAL offerings are available only with the Cloud Orchestrator deployment model; contact your local Fortinet
representative to evaluate the on-premises virtual appliance deployment.
What are the limitations in the EVAL versions of the FortiSASE Sovereign Orchestrator and User licenses?
The FortiSASE Sovereign EVAL Orchestrator is limited to a maximum of 100 EVAL users, and works with ‘SSNFR’ Fortigate
models only. This EVAL version of the FortiSASE Sovereign Orchestrator is intended for evaluation and demo purposes only and
is not intended for use in production environments.
How does the user licensing work in the EVAL version ?
The FortiSASE Sovereign EVAL Orchestrator supports a maximum of 100 user licenses. User licensing is sold in packs of 25, with
a minimum order quantity of 1 (25 total users). The maximum order quantity of FortiSASE Sovereign User License is 4 (100 total).

FortiSASE Sovereign Ordering Guide
What is different about the SSNFR FortiGate models compared to production?
FortiSASE Sovereign Not For Resale (SSNFR) SKUs are intended to be used with the FortiSASE Sovereign EVAL Orchestrator for
evaluation and demo purposes only. They are not intended to be used in production environments.
FortiGate ‘SSNFR’ models are fully functional FortiGate devices and include 1 year security inspection entitlement for the
FortiSASE Sovereign EVAL service.
