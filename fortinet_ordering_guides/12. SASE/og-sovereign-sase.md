FortiSASE Sovereign
FortiSASE Sovereign is a turnkey solution for customers who aim to deliver private SASE
services for internal or external consumption. It is designed for large enterprises, federal
agencies, and service providers looking to deliver private SASE services.
FortiSASE Sovereign provides an alternative to public SASE offerings, such as FortiSASE,
which are hosted in vendor datacenters. In contrast, FortiSASE Sovereign is designed for
deployment on customer-controlled infrastructure, ensuring that security inspection and
data sovereignty remain entirely within your organization’s boundaries and control.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS – BETA PROGRAM
How do I get started in the beta program?
The FortiSASE Sovereign beta program is free to join with FortiSASE Sovereign full or EVAL license subscription. It is
recommended to register with the following EVAL and NFR equipment:
• FortiSASE Sovereign Orchestration EVAL License: FC-10-OVSAN-1081-02-DD
• FortiSASE Sovereign 25 User License: FC-10-EMSSN-552-02-DD (Quantity of 4 max)
• FortiGate 91G or 901G (SSNFR version): FG-91G-SSNFR or FG-901G-SSNFR
• Any FortiAnalyzer appliance with firmware 7.4 or above
Once you have the appropriate equipment, you may register on FNDN using the link below to activate and get started:
FortiSASE Sovereign Beta Program
What are the limitation in the EVAL versions of the FortiSASE Sovereign Orchestrator and User licenses?
The FortiSASE Sovereign EVAL Orchestrator is limited to a maximum of 100 EVAL users, and works with ‘SSNFR’ Fortigate
models only. This EVAL version of the FortiSASE Sovereign Orchestrator is intended for evaluation and demo purposes only, and
not intended to be used in production environments.
How does the user licensing work in the EVAL version ?
The FortiSASE Sovereign EVAL Orchestrator supports a maximum of 100 user licenses. User licensing is sold in packs of 25, with
a minimum order quantity of 1 (25 total users). The maximum order quantity of FortiSASE Sovereign User License is 4 (100 total).
What is different about the SSNFR FortiGate models compared to production?
FortiSASE Sovereign Not For Resale (SSNFR) SKUs are intended to be used with the FortiSASE Sovereign EVAL Orchestrator for
evaluation and demo purposes only. They are not intended to be used in production environments.
FortiGate ‘SSNFR’ models are fully functional FortiGate devices and include 1 year security inspection entitlement for the
FortiSASE Sovereign EVAL service.
Who is allowed to participate in the beta?
The FortiSASE Sovereign Beta program is currently open to all partners and interested customers with a valid FNDN account.
Simply click the link below and click ‘Enroll’.

Where do I go for support and documentation once enrolled in the FortiSASE Sovereign beta?
All documentation, support and feedback will be included in the ‘Resources’ section of the beta program. This is a private
community with other enrolled beta users and the place to ask questions, retrieve documentation, and provide feedback to the
FortiSASE Sovereign team.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - FORTISASE SOVEREIGN
How does FortiSASE Sovereign compare to FortiSASE?
FortiSASE Sovereign is designed for customers who wish to offering private SASE for internal or external consumption. This
is accomplished by hosting the security inspection and data storage in customer-controlled infrastructure, such as private or
rented datacenters. By contrast, FortiSASE is hosted and managed by Fortinet with a vast, distributed cloud security network
and global POP presence.
Review the following Responsibility Matrix for further details regarding operational responsibilities of a public SASE offering
versus FortiSASE Sovereign.

Is FortiSASE Sovereign a SASE service?
FortiSASE Sovereign is not a standalone SASE service. It is designed as a turnkey solution to enable an organization or service
provider to provider their own, customizable SASE services.
What SASE features are available in FortiSASE Sovereign compared to FortiSASE?
FortiSASE Sovereign is based on FortiSASE and will have similar capabilities and feature set. Due to the private design of
FortiSASE Sovereign, some features will not be available at launch, including:
• DEM
• RBI
• CASB (API)
• Agentless ZTNA
• Cloud DLP
• SOCaaS
• FortiGuard Forensics
For roadmap questions and upcoming features, please contact your local account team. All roadmap items are subject to
change.
Where is the FortiSASE Sovereign controller hosted?
At launch the FortiSASE Sovereign controller will be hosted in Fortinet datacenters across several regions, including North
America and Europe.

Sovereign cloud orchestrator and web portal. FortiGates provide security inspection for the storage and archiving ATP licenses for endpoint security and
FortiSASE Sovereign end users connectivity
All four components are required for the FortiSASE Sovereign offering.
Can FortiSASE Sovereign work with FortiSASE in a hybrid solution?
No, FortiSASE Sovereign cannot be combined or used with FortiSASE.
It is designed as private solution, to be hosted in customer-controlled infrastructure. Customers who wish to deliver their own
private SASE service should ensure they have the infrastructure capacity to host the security inspection and data sovereignty
appliance. For customers who do not wish to host any appliance, Fortinet offers hosted appliance solutions, such as FortiGate
and FortiAnalyzer, that could be rented on a monthly basis.
What components do I need to host at my location?
There are two components that will be hosted at the customer premise:
• FortiGate Appliance for Security Inspection
• FortiAnalyzer Appliance for Data Storage and Sovereignty
The security inspection is powered by FortiGate which could be hosted in private datacenter locations or Fortinet datacenters
using FGaaS.
The FortiAnalyzer is designed to be hosted in the customer premise, within the boundaries of your data residency requirements.
Could the FortiGate (security inspection appliance) be hosted in Fortinet datacenters?
FortiGate-as-a-Service (FGaaS), is the industry’s first Hardware-as-a-Service option that combines the power of our proprietary
ASIC technology with the flexibility and scalability of cloud services. This service can be used with FortiSASE Sovereign to utilize
Fortinet resources for security inspection. FortiAnalyzer-as-a-Service will be available in a future release date.
Who controls the design and architecture of FortiGate and Security Fabric devices?
While Fortinet provides best practice design and topologies, we give the customer autonomy over their design and deployment
for their SASE service. Customers could choose how many FortiGates to deploy according to their intra-datacenter and inter-
datacenter failover requirements.
