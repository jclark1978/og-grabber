Ordering Guide
FortiEDR
FortiEDR is Fortinet’s advanced endpoint detection and response solution designed to
detect and neutralize threats in real time, both pre- and post-execution. It helps reduce
dwell time, automate incident response, and extend protection across the entire attack
surface using AI-driven behavior analysis, threat hunting, and orchestration.
This solution is ideal for organizations that need a modern approach to endpoint
protection, forensic visibility, and coordinated response—integrated tightly with the
Fortinet Security Fabric.
Key Highlights:
• Comprehensive EDR/XDR Coverage: Detect and respond across endpoints with
features like continuous monitoring, AI tagging, and behavior-based analytics.
• Flexible Service Options: Available as self-managed or fully managed (MDR) with
optional on-premises deployments.
• Seamless Integration: Natively integrates with FortiGate, FortiAnalyzer, and third-party
tools for full visibility and automated remediation.
• Designed for Real-World Use Cases: Supports various tiers including discover-only,
full EDR, or extended XDR, based on customer maturity and goals.
• Scalable for All Sizes: Available in 25, 500, 2,000, and 10,000-pack licenses.
Deployment & Licensing Notes:
• Tiers: FortiEDR is offered in Discover & Protect (EPP-Light), Full EDR, and XDR tiers.
Managed options are available at all levels.
• BPS Requirement: FortiCare Best Practice Service (BPS) is mandatory for all new
deployments that include EDR or XDR functionality.
• Cloud & On-prem Support: FortiEDR supports both cloud-native and on-premises
deployments.
• Air-Gapped Deployment: FortiEDR supports fully isolated on-premises air-gapped
deployments for highly secure environments requiring no internet connectivity.
• Unified Agent Branding: FortiEDR is available standalone or through FortiEndpoint,
which includes the unified FortiClient agent.
Add-on Services Available:
• FortiCare BPS Onboarding (required): Size-based SKUs ranging from <500 to
30,000+ endpoints
• Professional Services: Forensics, IR training, consulting, and implementation
• Training: FortiEDR NSE5, FCP Certification, Virtual ILT, and labs
• Cloud Storage Expansion: 512 GB storage add-on (FC-10-FEDR1-1112-01-DD)

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the easiest way to order?
License packs of 25, 500, 2,000, and 10,000 are available for terms of 1-5 years. Refer to the ordering example on the previous
page for expansions, renewals, and upgrades.
Does FortiEDR have a minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
The MOQ is 500 seats for all bundles except for the FortiEDR Discover & Protect, the 350 family of SKUs which allows 100 seats,
the FortiEDR Discover & Protect and Basic MDR, the 391 family of SKUs which allows 100 seats, the FortiEDR Protect & Respond
and Standard MDR, the 392 family of SKUs which allows 100 seats and the FortiEDR Discover, Protect & Respond and Standard
MDR, the 349 family of SKUs which allows 100 seats. That said, positioning 500 seats for smaller seat accounts who can
manage their incident response is a valid option.
Can I mix bundles?
No. FortiEDR supports a single bundle per customer account.
Can existing customers upgrade bundles?
Yes. Customers can upgrade at any point of time: mid-term or upon renewal.
What if my customer is a managed security service provider (MSSP)?
Checking the MSSP Ordering Guide is recommended. MSSP consumption plans are available on an annual or monthly billing
basis.
What services can be provided to an on-premise environment?
On-premise hosted environments require Fortinet Cloud Services Internet connectivity at all times. Once connected, all
FortiEDR-related services can be delivered remotely, except for MDR service. FortiEDR also supports air-gapped isolated on-
premise-hosted environments.
What is FortiEndpoint?
FortiEndpoint is Fortinet’s unified agent that combines FortiClient and FortiEDR. It provides a comprehensive solution through
a single SKU that includes secure connectivity options (VPN or ZTNA), as well as Endpoint Protection Platform (EPP), Endpoint
Detection and Response (EDR), and Extended Detection and Response (XDR) capabilities.
For more information, please visit FortiEndpoint Ordering Guide.
