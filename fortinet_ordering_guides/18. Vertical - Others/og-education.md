Fortinet Cyber Safety Resources
The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) stresses the need for educational institutions to be proactive
in protecting their networks and systems against cyber threats, as students, faculty, and staff increasingly rely on technology
inside and outside the classroom.
CISA’s recommendations align with programs in other countries such as the European Union’s NIS2 Directive, the UK’s Cyber
Essentials program, Saudi Arabia’s National Cyber Security Authority’s Essential Cybersecurity Controls, and recommendations
derived from LATAM’s Cyber Summit Annual Report, as well as many more countries. At a high level, CISA recommends that
educational institutions enhance their cybersecurity posture in the following areas:
• Developing and implementing a comprehensive cybersecurity program that includes policies, procedures, and technical
controls to protect sensitive information and prevent cyber incidents
• Providing regular cybersecurity awareness training to students, teachers, and staff to help them identify and prevent cyber
threats
• Implementing strong authentication methods, such as multi-factor authentication, to protect against unauthorized access to
sensitive information
• Collaborating with local law enforcement and other stakeholders to ensure a coordinated response in the event of a cyber
incident

Content Filtering: On-Campus and Off-Campus Cyber Safety
Content filtering plays a crucial role in enhancing student cyber safety by helping to safeguard against inappropriate, harmful, or
distracting online content. Given the vast amount of information available on the internet, students are exposed to risks such as
cyberbullying, explicit content, and potentially harmful websites.
FortiGate and FortiClient offer a seamless solution for coordinating content filtering, regardless of whether students are on or
off campus, by utilizing the same FortiGuard content filtering categories. These categories can be configured and applied using
age-appropriate profiles or policies and may differ for on-campus and off-campus use. Content profiles can be configured in
FortiOS and synchronized to FortiClient EMS for ease of management.
