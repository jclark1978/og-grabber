FortiEdge Cloud
Overview
FortiEdge Cloud is a powerful, cloud-based management platform designed to streamline the deployment,
configuration, and monitoring of Fortinet’s LAN and WAN Edge portfolio, including FortiSwitch, FortiAP,
and FortiExtender. With its intuitive and user-friendly interface, FortiEdge Cloud enables zero-touch
deployment, centralized configuration management, comprehensive reporting, and insightful analytics for
standalone deployments.
Offering unparalleled flexibility and accessibility, FortiEdge Cloud empowers you to manage your branch
network from anywhere, at any time. Its scalable architecture seamlessly accommodates growth,
effortlessly scaling from a small number of devices to thousands across multiple sites. Whether you’re
overseeing a modest network or a vast, distributed infrastructure, FortiEdge Cloud provides a streamlined
and efficient solution for comprehensive network management.
Order Information
FortiEdge Cloud offers a flexible and scalable pricing model to cater to diverse needs. The platform
provides a limited Freemium tier, allowing users to experience its core functionalities at no cost. For those
seeking advanced enterprise-grade features and comprehensive management capabilities, FortiEdge
Cloud offers a fully licensed tier that unlocks the complete suite of powerful tools and resources.
With the Freemium tier, users can explore the platform essential features and gain a hands-on
understanding of its capabilities. This introductory offering serves as an ideal starting point for
organizations considering FortiEdge Cloud or those with modest requirements.
For organizations with more complex and demanding network environments, the fully licensed tier
delivers an extensive array of enterprise-grade features and unparalleled management capabilities. This
comprehensive solution empowers IT teams with advanced tools, robust reporting, and granular control
over their LAN and WAN edge infrastructure, enabling them to optimize performance, ensure compliance,
and drive operational efficiency across their distributed networks.
