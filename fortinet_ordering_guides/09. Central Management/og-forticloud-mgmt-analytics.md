FortiCloud SaaS
Management & Analytics
As a unified SaaS platform, FortiCloud ensures comprehensive oversight and enhanced
control across your entire security landscape. Seamless and scalable, FortiCloud can
accelerate deployment, optimize operations, and maintain robust security all through a
streamlined cloud-based interface designed for efficiency and ease of use.
Benefits of cloud-based management and analytics:
1. Elimination of expensive upfront management infrastructure costs allowing you to save
IT resources
2. Highly redundant and secure architecture with 24x7 support that reduces disruptions
and ensures business continuity
3. A pay-as-you-grow model that lets you scale from small installations to larger
deployments
4. Multitenancy with complete tenant isolation so service providers can manage multiple
tenants with confidence

FortiGate Cloud Ordering FAQ
1. What is the difference between FortiGate Cloud subscription tiers?
FortiGate Cloud subscriptions are designed for organizations looking for an intuitive cloud experience with comprehensive
management and analytics capabilities to simplify your operations.
For more information, please see https://docs.fortinet.com/document/fortigate-cloud/latest/administration-guide/215425/
feature-comparison.
2. How do I upgrade from a FortiGate Cloud basic tier to a standard tier?
Simply purchase the FortiGate Cloud standard tier subscription (FC-10-xxxxx-131-02-DD) to unlock the full set of features and
benefits.
3. Is the FortiGate Cloud subscription per firewall device?
Yes. One subscription is required for each FortiGate or FortiWiFi firewall being managed in FortiGate Cloud. Therefore,
purchasing 10 FortiGate firewalls requires 10 corresponding FortiGate Cloud subscriptions.
4. Does FortiGate Cloud offer zero-touch provisioning (ZTP)?
FortiGates can be imported and provisioned to cloud with FortiCloud key or bulk key(FortiDeploy single use SKU).
5. Is FortiDeploy a different subscription?
Yes, FortiDeploy is used for bulk deployment of FortiGates into FortiGate Cloud, and it is a separate subscription.
FortiDeploy FDP-SINGLE-USE Enables zero touch bulk provisioning for your FortiGate, FortiWifi, or FortiAP products.
6. Does FortiGate Cloud support FortiSwitch, FortiAP and FortiExtender?
FortiGate Cloud supports FortiSwitch, FortiAP and FortiExtender that are downstream connected to the FortiGate. For the
FortiSwitch and FortiAP, FortiGate acts as a wired and wireless controller.
7. Do we need separate subscriptions for FortiGate Cloud to manage FortiSwitch, FortiAP and FortiExtender?
Separate licenses are not needed to use FortiGate Cloud to manage these devices when they are downstream connected to
FortiGate.
8. We previously used SKU (FCLE-10-FCLDO-161-02-12) for sub-accounts-based multitenancy. What is the
recommendation for replacement?
This legacy sub-accounts-based multitenancy SKU has reached its end-of-life. Multitenancy is now supported via FortiCloud
Organizations. By default, all accounts can use multitenancy for up to 10 accounts. To enable unlimited accounts support,
register a FNDN basic account with the same email ID is required.
9. We use Legacy Multitenancy right now, will Fortinet stop supporting it?
Any customer with an existing, valid -161 license will be able to use it until the end of the license term. They will not be able to
renew it at the end of the term and will need to register FNDN basic account to use Organizations Multitenancy.

10. Does FortiGate Cloud need FortiCloud Premium?
No, to use Organizations multi-tenancy, FortiCloud Premium license is not required. You can register FNDN Basic Account with
same email id as Organizations account to enable full access to Organizations based multi-tenancy features.
11. How can I migrate my legacy multitenant structures to Organizations multitenancy?
Work with your Fortinet account team to help you in the migration.
12. We have FortiGate Cloud Advanced subscription (FC-10-xxxxx-1125-02-12) for SD-WAN Overlay-as-a-Service, but it is
not available for order. What is the recommended replacement?
We have introduced new SD-WAN bundles (FC-10-xxxxx-1337-02-DD and FC-10-xxxxx-1339-02-DD) which deliver
comprehensive SD-WAN capabilities and include both FortiGate Cloud standard subscription and SD-WAN Overlay-as-a-
Service.
13. Why are SD-WAN bundles not offered for FortiGate VM?
For FortiGate VM deployment, Fortinet continues offering services on an a-la-carte basis. To leverage SD-WAN Overlay-as-
a-Service, we recommend customers purchase FortiGate Cloud Advanced tier (FC1-10-xxxxx-1125-02-DD) that includes this
functionality.
