FortiGate-as-a-Service
Cloud
Overview
FortiGate-as-a-Service (FGaaS) delivers dedicated Fortinet hardware appliances as a
fully hosted cloud service—combining the high performance of physical devices with the
simplicity and scalability of the cloud. This service provides advanced security backed
by FortiGuard security services, routable IPs, and guaranteed bandwidth. Customers can
subscribe flexibly via monthly FortiPoint consumption or annual SKUs.

FortiGate-as-a-Service Ordering Guide
Hardware (Hosted Physical Appliances)
FortiGate (FGaaS)
Dedicated FortiGate hardware models entry-level to high-end running FortiOS with hardware-accelerated security, SD-WAN,
and FortiGuard services.
AI-Powered Security Service
Leverage Fortinet industry-leading threat intelligence powered by FortiGuard Labs, to provide real-time proactive protection
against known and emerging threats. Select from FortiGuard UTP or Enterprise Services Bundles, with a-la-carte services to
ensure you are protected from the latest threats
Cost Efficiency
• Helps avoid large upfront CapEx by enabling customers to deploy high-end FortiGate appliances in the cloud without
purchasing hardware, significantly reducing overall TCO.
• Eliminates multi-year hardware refresh cycles, end-of-support risks, and migration costs since Fortinet continuously provides
current and supported hardware minimizing lifecycle TCO.
• Removes customer-paid co-location space, power, cooling, remote hands, routers, cabling, and cross-connect infrastructure
by hosting everything in Fortinet-managed data centers, lowering operational TCO.
• Provides predictable, all-inclusive subscription pricing covering hardware, bandwidth, power, IP addresses, monitoring, and
FortiGuard services, reducing fragmented vendor spend and simplifying TCO planning.
• Allows elastic hardware scaling, enabling customers to begin with smaller models for PoCs and upgrade seamlessly as
demand grows avoiding overprovisioning and unnecessary TCO increases.
• Eliminates the need to maintain spare devices or inventory for HA/DR deployments, reducing storage, logistics, and
procurement overhead, further lowering TCO.
• Reduces operational workload by removing hardware supply chain tasks such as imports, RMA logistics, shipping delays, and
customs handling improving operational efficiency and TCO.
• Offers ASIC-accelerated Fortinet hardware performance at a fraction of the cost of purchasing large appliances outright,
optimizing cost-to-performance TCO.
• Supports OPEX-friendly consumption through FortiPoints, avoiding long-term lock-ins and enabling accurate TCO
predictability for variable or seasonal workloads.

FortiGate as a Service FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)
How do we access FGaaS service?
FGaaS is hosted in FortiCloud, and customers need to register in FortiCloud to get this service. You can navigate from FortiCloud
> FGaaS which leads to following portal:
https://fgaas.forticloud.com
Is there any standard SKU apart from FortiPoints?
Standard SKUs are supported for all hardware under the services. Also, standard SKUs can be purchased with FortiPoints.
How does the FortiPoints billing work?
Billing is done monthly with FortiPoints. This schedule provides customers with the ability to set up and use on-demand.
Monthly deductible FortiPoints are visible on the FGaaS portal.
What happens to my FortiPoints if I terminate the FortiGate mid-month?
The termination will be scheduled for the next billing date as it is not effective immediately.
How many FortiPoints do I need to get started?
Make use of FGaaS calculator from FNDN portal to see how many points are needed.
Does this service support multi-tenancy model on FortiCloud?
FortiCloud Organization level tenancy is supported.
Does the Partner need to supply Telco services for customers? Does it provide WAN links?
The service includes Public IPs and Bandwidth offering.
What are the supported data centers?
Visit Fortinet Docs site for all supported DCs:
https://docs.fortinet.com/document/fortigate-as-a-service/latest/administration-guide/790753/introduction
Is there a limitation or minimum requirements?
Total rentable FortiGate per customer: 2
Maximum bandwidth per FortiGate: 10Gbps Shared
Total IP addresses per FortiGate: 5
Two IP addresses are minimum requirements; while one IP address is dedicated for management the other is used for production
traffic.
If you reach the service quota limit, open a Service Request from FGaaS portal.
How is HA configured, connected, and managed?
HA devices are pre-cabled in the data center and automatically provisioned through the FGaaS portal, ensuring seamless
redundancy and maximum uptime.

How is hardware replacement handled?
Since the hardware is subscribed and not owned, the process is called Device Replacement rather than RMA. If a hardware
issue is detected, a replacement device is quickly provided to ensure service continuity keeping the experience smooth and
disruption free.
What is the SLA for the service?
The official SLA is detailed in the Service Description document available under the “Guidelines and Policies” section in the
FortiCloud portal.
What are the best use cases for the service?
FortiGate: SD-WAN Hub, Sovereign SASE inspection gateway, IoT/OT edge protection, Wireless LAN controller, Secure Web
Gateway, and any typical on-prem FortiGate deployment using the same licenses and full hardware performance.
