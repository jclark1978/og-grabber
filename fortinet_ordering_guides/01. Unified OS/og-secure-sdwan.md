# og-secure-sdwan

Ordering Guide
Secure SD-WAN
The Secure SD-WAN Ordering Guide is a complete reference for choosing and ordering Fortinet
SD-WAN solutions. It is designed to support a wide range of deployment needs—from small
branch offices to large enterprise hubs—with detailed product specifications, licensing details, and
deployment guidance.
Key highlights of this guide include:
• Comprehensive Appliance Listings: Covers branch and hub devices with performance metrics
(IPsec VPN, Threat Protection, SSL Inspection, etc.).
• Flexible Deployment Options: Includes WiFi, storage, DSL, PoE, cellular variants, and support for
3G/4G/5G connectivity.
• SD-WAN Service Bundle (1329 / 1337) Licensing
• NEW: SD-WAN Service Add-on (1387 / 1389) Licensing
• Centralized Management: Details on FortiManager platforms, VM/cloud compatibility, and
subscription tiers.
• Virtual and Cloud Support: Compatibility matrices for private and public cloud platforms (AWS,
Azure, VMware, etc.).
• Professional Services: QuickStart deployment options and training resources to fast-track your
SD-WAN rollout.
• Training: Information about the latest courses and certifications available for SD-WAN
administrators.
• Selection Guidance: Criteria for choosing the right branch or hub device based on users,
throughput, connectivity, and redundancy.

How are SDWAN Services procured? Is a FortiManager license required?
SD-WAN Services are licensed on a per-FortiGate basis. For FortiGate models smaller than a FortiGate 60G, the bundle is
procured using the 1337 SKU prefix, and the add-on uses the 1887 SKU prefix. For FortiGate 60G and larger models, the bundle
is procured using the 1329 SKU prefix, and the add-on uses the 1889 SKU prefix.
A FortiManager license is not required to procure or use the SD-WAN Service bundle.
Can I still procure the older SD-WAN, SASE or Attack Surface SKUs al a carte?
No. As of Jan 2026, the individual SKUs will no longer be available for sale individually, and can only be procured through the
SDWAN Service Bundle.
Is FortiCare included?
FortiCare is included in the standalone SD-WAN services bundles (SKU 1329 and 1337). FortiCare is not included with the SD-
WAN add-on SKUs (1387 / 1389)
For Network Overlay provisioning and management, which solutions require a license?
Fortinet offers three solutions for SD-WAN network overlay provisioning and management:
1. Fabric Overlay Orchestrator: Free; included in FortiOS 7.2 and above.
2. FortiManager Overlay Orchestrator: Included with FortiManager 7.2 and above.
3. FortiGate Cloud’s Overlay-as-a-Service: License required; included in the SD-WAN Service Bundle

How is “Threat Protection” measured and what does it include?
Threat Protection performance is measured with Firewall, IPS, Application Control, URL Filtering and Malware Protection
enabled, utilizing “Enterprise Mix” traffic profile.
