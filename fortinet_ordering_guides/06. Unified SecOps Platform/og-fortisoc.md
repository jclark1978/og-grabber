Frequently Asked Questions
What are the FortiSOC subscription tiers?
FortiSOC offers two subscription tiers based on daily ingestion volume (GB/day):
• Base Tier: Entry-level option designed for SMB and mid-market customers. Includes core SIEM analytics, dashboards, and
alerting.
• Advanced Tier: For enterprises and MSSPs. Includes everything in the Base tier plus advanced SIEM and SOAR automation
capabilities.
How are the licenses measured?
Both tiers are licensed per GB/day of log ingestion.
For example, 10 GB/day means the platform can ingest up to 10 GB of logs per day.
What retention is included by default?
Both Base and Advanced licenses include 1 month of analytics retention by default.
How do I extend analytics retention?
Use the FC1-10-FSCLD-1311-02-DD add-on.
Each unit adds 1 additional month of analytics retention per GB/day licensed.
Retention add-ons can be applied to either Base or Advanced tiers.
Example: For 10 GB/day with 3 months retention → 2 extra months × 10 GB = 20 units of FC1-10-FSCLD-1311-02-DD
How do I order FortiSOC Base or Advanced licenses?
• Base Tier: Order FC1-10-FSCLD-1310-02-DD × [number of GB/day]
• Advanced Tier: Order FC1-10-FSCLD-1318-02-DD × [number of GB/day]
• Add optional analytic retention with FC1-10-FSCLD-1311-02-DD × [extra months × GB/day]
• Add optional archive retention with FC1-10-FSCLD-1312-02-DD × [extra months × GB/day]
Example:
For 10 GB/day Advanced tier with 3 months analytic retention:
FC1-10-FSCLD-1318-02-DD × 10
FC1-10-FSCLD-1311-02-DD × 20
Can customers upgrade from Base to Advanced?
Yes. You can upgrade anytime by co-term to the Advanced tier license for the same GB/day volume. The new license replaces
the Base tier entitlement and activates additional SIEM + SOAR features.
Can retention be ordered separately?
No. Retention add-ons must be attached to an active Base or Advanced license.
What if the customer’s log ingestion grows?
They can add more GB/day units under the same tier by using co-term
Both Base and Advanced licenses scale linearly with ingestion volume.

Are the pricing structures different between tiers?
Yes. The Base tier is priced lower to provide an affordable entry option. The Advanced tier has a higher rate reflecting the added
full SIEM correlation, automation, and orchestration capabilities.
Do both tiers share the same license term?
Yes. All SKUs (Base, Advanced, Retention) align under the same subscription term — typically 1 year.
