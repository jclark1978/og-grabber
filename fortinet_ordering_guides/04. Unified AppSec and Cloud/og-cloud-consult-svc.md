Cloud Consulting Services
Cloud Security, Networking, and DevOps Consulting
Services
Fortinet’s Cloud Consulting Services provide expertise in securing cloud environments
through security posture assessments, architecture development and implementation,
and provisioning automation offerings tailored solutions for the needs of each client.
We offer three unique value propositions, including our global presence, a vendor-
agnostic approach, and our commitment to transferring knowledge to the customer
throughout our engagements. The team focuses on adhering to best practices of scale,
performance, high availability, security, operational excellence, and cost optimization in
all our methodology and deliverables.
Cloud Consulting Service Offerings Overview
Our cloud services are divided into Consultation and Execution Services to
comprehensively address diverse infrastructure and security needs across all phases of
cloud adoption. Our service catalog is designed to support clients’ cloud journey from
strategy through to execution.
