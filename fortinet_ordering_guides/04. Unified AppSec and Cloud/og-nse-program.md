DEVELOPING EXPERTS IN THE FIELD OF CYBERSECURITY
This ordering guide is a quick reference for the Fortinet NSE Training and Certification
Program.
The Fortinet Network Security Expert (NSE) Certification program has been carefully
crafted and researched over several years to address the rapidly evolving threat
landscape as well as job-market needs. Our primary objective is to satisfy both current
and future requirements, ensuring that the NSE Certification program remains at the
forefront of network security expertise.
The certification program includes a wide range of free self-paced courses and
instructor-led courses, as well as practical,
hands-on exercises that demonstrate mastery of complex cybersecurity concepts. Each
certification program assesses specific levels of cybersecurity expertise, from foundation
to architect.

Frequently Asked Questions
How can I prepare for NSE certification?
Choose self-paced e-learning, instructor-led training, or hands-on labs to gain Fortinet expertise.
What are the training options?
• Self-paced learning (self-study)—Flexible online training with video content
• Instructor-led training (ILT)—Expert-guided, interactive sessions available online or onsite with course materials and labs
• On-demand lab access—Hands-on virtual labs for real-world troubleshooting and configuration
• Private training packages—Customizable training to align with business security objectives and compliance needs
• Certification exam vouchers—Available for bulk purchases; ideal for enterprises upskilling multiple employees
How do I register for free self-paced training?
All self-paced lessons in the Fortinet Training Institute Library are free of charge. On-demand labs are not included.
Learn about: How do I register for the free online self-paced training?
How do I purchase Fortinet-delivered ILT courses?
Effective February 3rd, 2025, all training SKUs are now based on course duration, to simplify the ordering of Fortinet training.
Learn about: Purchasing Process and How do I purchase Fortinet-delivered instructor-led courses using a credit card?
How do I redeem my Fortinet ILT training voucher?
Once you’ve purchased your voucher, you can redeem it and self-enroll on the Fortinet Training Institute Schedule web page.
Learn about: How do I redeem my Fortinet instructor-led training voucher?
What is on-demand lab access?
Practice configuring Fortinet solutions in a virtual setup.
Learn about: What is on-demand lab access within self-paced courses?
What is the difference between ILT private and public sessions?
• What are Fortinet NSE ILT private sessions?
• What are Fortinet NSE ILT public sessions?
How can I buy exam vouchers?
Learn about: Exam Voucher Pricing for NSE Certifications
What is the difference between the NSE-EX-FTE2 and NSE-EX-FTE4 exam vouchers?
The main difference is the certification exams they can be used for:
• NSE-EX-FTE2 ($200) – Valid for Fortinet NSE 4, NSE 5, and NSE 6 certification exams.
• NSE-EX-FTE4 ($400) – Valid for Fortinet NSE 7 certification exams and NSE 8 recertification exams.
Each exam requires one voucher.
Do I need NSE 4 before taking NSE 5 or NSE 6?
Yes. NSE 4 is a mandatory prerequisite for NSE 5 and NSE 6 certification.
How long is the certification valid?
All NSE certifications are valid for 2 years.
Does passing NSE 5 or NSE 6 renew NSE 4?
No. NSE 4 must be maintained separately.
Which certifications are renewed when I pass NSE 7?
Passing NSE 7 will renew:
• NSE 1–4
• NSE 5 and NSE 6 within the same track

Does NSE 8 renew all certifications?
No. Maintaining NSE 8 does not automatically renew at all levels.
Lower-level certifications are renewed through NSE 7.
How do I renew NSE 4 to NSE 7 certifications?
You have two options:
• Retake the exam
• Complete a recertification assessment (if available)
Click for more information: What options will I have to renew certifications?
What is the recertification assessment?
A shorter online course with an assessment covering updated content.
Available within the first 2 years after certification.
What are the recertification assessments?
What happens if I don’t renew within 2 years?
You must take the full exam again.
How does the renewal cycle work?
Years 0–2 → Recertification assessment (if available)
After 2 years → Full exam required
How can I renew my NSE 8 certification?
You have two options:
• Retake the practical exam every 2 years
• Accumulate 200 certification points (new system)
Click the link for more information: How do I renew my NSE 8 certification?
What are certification points for NSE 8?
Certification points can be earned through approved activities and used to maintain NSE 8 without retaking the exam.

What are the SKUs for NSE training?
Instructor-led training (in-person or virtual): FT-ILT-DXX
Example: FT-ILT-D02 = 2-day standard training.
Self-paced, on-demand labs: FT-LAB-HXX
Example: FT-LAB-H20 = 20-hour lab session.
What are the SKUs for custom workshops?
Custom content tailored to specific customer needs: FT-CTM-DXX
Example: FT-CTM-D03 = 3-day custom training session
What are add-ons for private sessions?
Onsite delivery, shipment fees, custom courses, private recordings, and additional fees.
Example: FT-ONSITE, FT-RECORD, FT-GUIDE, FT-SHIP, and so on.
When should I use the SKU FT-CTM-DXX?
When custom content training is required. A scoping meeting and project ID are needed. Submit a ticket through the Help Desk.
Which SKU should I use in the following scenario?

SKU: 10 x FT-ILT-D02 + 10 x FT-ILT-D01
Additional SKU: 1 x FT-ONSITE for onsite delivery
